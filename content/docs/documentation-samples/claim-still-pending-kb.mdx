# Why Is My Claim Still Pending?

**Applies to:** Members, Customer Service Representatives  
**Last reviewed:** March 2026

If your claim has been in **Pending** status longer than expected, this article explains the most common reasons and what you can do.

## Expected processing times

| Claim type | Standard processing | Notes |
| ---------- | ------------------- | ----- |
| Clean claim (electronic) | 5–7 business days | No additional info needed |
| Paper claim | 15–30 business days | Mailing and manual entry add time |
| Claim requiring review | 10–15 business days | Medical necessity, out-of-network |
| Appeal in progress | 30 calendar days | See [Appeals workflow](/prodoc/documentation-samples/implementation-appeals) |

<Note title="Business days">
Processing times are measured in business days (Monday–Friday, excluding federal holidays). Claims submitted on Friday evening may not enter processing until the following Monday.
</Note>

## Common reasons for pending status

### 1. Missing or incomplete information

The most common cause. The claim may be waiting for:

- **Provider tax ID (TIN)** — Does not match our provider directory
- **Diagnosis codes (ICD-10)** — Missing or incompatible with procedure codes
- **Prior authorization number** — Required for certain procedures but not included
- **Member ID mismatch** — ID on the claim does not match enrollment records

**What you can do:** Check the claim details in the member portal under **My Claims**. If a document request appears, upload the requested information promptly.

### 2. Coordination of Benefits (COB)

If you have coverage through more than one plan (e.g., your plan and a spouse's plan), we must determine which plan pays first. This process adds 5–10 business days.

**What you can do:** Ensure your COB information is up to date in the member portal under **Profile → Other Coverage**. Upload your other plan's Explanation of Benefits (EOB) if requested.

### 3. Provider verification

We may need to verify that the provider was in-network and credentialed on the date of service. This is common for:

- New providers not yet in our directory
- Out-of-network claims requiring additional review
- Providers with recently updated credentials

**What you can do:** No action needed from you unless we send a document request. Processing typically resumes within 3–5 business days.

### 4. Duplicate claim check

When a similar claim was recently submitted, our system holds the new claim for duplicate review. This prevents double payment but adds 2–3 business days.

**What you can do:** If you intentionally resubmitted a corrected claim, no action is needed. The system will release it after review.

### 5. System processing backlog

During peak periods (January enrollment changes, year-end), processing volumes increase and timelines may extend by 2–5 business days.

**What you can do:** Check the **System Status** page for any active processing delays. No individual action required.

## How to check your claim status

1. Log in to the member portal
2. Navigate to **My Claims**
3. Select the claim to view its timeline and current status
4. Look for any **Action Required** banners indicating missing information

## When to contact Member Services

Contact us if:

- Your claim has been pending for **more than 30 business days** (electronic) or **45 business days** (paper)
- You received a document request but are unsure what to provide
- The claim status has not changed in **14 or more business days**
- You believe the claim was submitted incorrectly and need to resubmit

**Member Services:** 1-800-555-0142 (Mon–Fri, 8 AM – 8 PM ET)

When calling, have your **member ID** and **claim number** ready.

## For Customer Service Representatives

| Pending reason code | Description | CSR action |
| ------------------- | ----------- | ---------- |
| `PND-001` | Missing diagnosis codes | Request corrected claim from provider |
| `PND-002` | COB review in progress | Verify other coverage on file |
| `PND-003` | Provider verification | Escalate to Provider Relations if &gt; 5 days |
| `PND-004` | Duplicate check | Review original claim; release or deny |
| `PND-005` | Prior auth required | Check auth system; request retro auth if eligible |
| `PND-006` | Manual review queue | Note estimated review date for member |

<Tip title="Set expectations">
Always provide the member with the specific pending reason and estimated resolution date from the claim timeline. Avoid generic "still processing" responses.
</Tip>
