# How to Submit a Grievance Form

Use this guide to file a **grievance** when you are dissatisfied with the quality of care, customer service, or access to benefits — separate from an appeal of a denied claim.

**Estimated time:** 10–15 minutes  
**Who can use this:** Plan members and authorized representatives

<Note title="Grievance vs. appeal">
A **grievance** addresses service quality or access issues. An **appeal** challenges a specific claim denial. If your issue involves a denied claim, see [Why Is My Claim Still Pending?](/prodoc/documentation-samples/claim-still-pending-kb) or contact Member Services to initiate an appeal.
</Note>

## Before you begin

Gather the following information:

- Member ID (found on your insurance card)
- Date(s) of the incident
- Provider or facility name (if applicable)
- Brief description of the issue
- Supporting documents (optional): photos, correspondence, medical records

## Steps

### 1. Log in to the member portal

Navigate to `https://members.your-healthplan.com` and sign in with your username and password.

<Screenshot
  alt="Member portal login page with username and password fields"
  caption="Figure 1 — Sign in with the credentials you created during enrollment."
/>

If you have not registered, select **Create Account** and verify your identity using your member ID and date of birth.

### 2. Open the Grievances section

From the dashboard, select **Grievances & Complaints** in the left navigation menu.

<Screenshot
  alt="Member portal dashboard with Grievances and Complaints highlighted in navigation"
  caption="Figure 2 — The Grievances section is under Member Services."
/>

### 3. Start a new grievance

Click **Submit New Grievance**. Review the grievance rights notice and select **I Understand, Continue**.

### 4. Complete the grievance form

Fill in each required field:

| Field | What to enter |
| ----- | ------------- |
| Grievance type | Select the category that best describes your issue (e.g., Customer Service, Quality of Care, Access to Care) |
| Date of incident | The date the issue occurred or when you became aware of it |
| Provider/facility | Name of the provider or facility involved, if applicable |
| Description | Describe what happened in your own words (minimum 50 characters) |
| Desired resolution | What outcome you are seeking |

<Tip title="Be specific">
Include dates, names, and claim or reference numbers if you have them. Specific details help us investigate faster.
</Tip>

### 5. Attach supporting documents (optional)

Click **Add Attachment** to upload up to 5 files (PDF, JPG, PNG; max 10 MB each). Common attachments include:

- Screenshots of portal errors
- Letters from your provider
- Call logs or email correspondence

### 6. Review and submit

Review your entries on the confirmation screen. Check the attestation box confirming the information is accurate, then click **Submit Grievance**.

<Screenshot
  alt="Grievance confirmation screen showing summary and Submit button"
  caption="Figure 3 — Review all fields before submitting. You cannot edit after submission."
/>

## Expected result

After submission:

1. You receive a **confirmation email** with your grievance reference number (format: `GRV-2026-XXXXX`)
2. The grievance appears in your portal under **My Grievances** with status **Received**
3. A Customer Advocate will acknowledge your grievance within **5 business days**
4. A resolution decision will be issued within **30 calendar days** (or 72 hours for urgent grievances)

You can track status updates in the portal and will receive email notifications at each stage.

## FAQ

**Can someone submit a grievance on my behalf?**

Yes. Authorized representatives (legal guardians, power of attorney holders) can submit on your behalf. They must be registered as an authorized user on your account.

**What if I already called Member Services about this issue?**

You can still file a formal grievance. Phone complaints are logged separately; a written grievance triggers the formal review process with documented timelines.

**Can I submit more than one grievance about the same issue?**

No. If you already have an open grievance for the same incident, the system will prompt you to add information to the existing case instead of creating a duplicate.

**How do I escalate if I am not satisfied with the resolution?**

If you disagree with the grievance resolution, you may request an **external review** by contacting your state's Department of Insurance. Instructions are included in the resolution letter.
