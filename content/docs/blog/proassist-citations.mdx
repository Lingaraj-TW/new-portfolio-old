# ProAssist citation-based answers

Generic chatbots hallucinate. **ProAssist** answers from your documentation corpus and cites the exact page and heading for every claim.

## Why citations matter

Regulated teams—insurance, payments, healthcare—need audit trails. Citations let reviewers verify AI output against published source material.

## How it works

1. Reader asks a question in the assistant panel.
2. ProAssist retrieves relevant MDX sections via hybrid search.
3. The response includes inline links to source docs.
4. Unanswered queries feed ProInsights gap reports.

## Best practices

- Keep headings descriptive for accurate citation anchors.
- Cross-link related procedures so retrieval surfaces complete workflows.
- Review gap reports weekly to author missing topics.
