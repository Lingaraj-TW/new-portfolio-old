# Contribute

## What to contribute

- Fix unclear steps and broken flows
- Add missing examples, edge cases, and troubleshooting
- Improve structure and navigation

## How contributions connect to the loop

- ProFeed identifies friction
- ProInsights prioritizes what matters most
- Contributions update ProDoc content
